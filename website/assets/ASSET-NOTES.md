- Head office location: Indangan, Davao City (slide 7).
- Headlines/navigation: Manrope, weights 700/600/500 (slides 52-54).
- Body/digital interfaces: Source Sans 3, 400/600 (slides 52-54). Not found among local installed user or system fonts during this extraction.
- Primary colors: RRC Navy #113B6A; Deep Navy #0B2A49; Blueprint Blue #4676A8; Signal Blue #2EA7E0; Warm Foundation Ivory #F6F0E7 (slide 49).
- Support: Future Gold #E7A33A; Structural Steel #53616A; Architectural Silver #C3CCD1; Graphite #11161C; Ink #16212B; White #FFFFFF (slide 50).
- Status: Red #B3261E; Green #287A57 (slide 50).
- White normal text on Signal Blue and Future Gold fails the brand's stated contrast requirement; use Deep Navy there (slide 51).
- Logo minimum: full-color and one-color master 140 digital px; small format 96 digital px (slide 44). The book reuses the same one-color image as its small-format example.
- Logo clear space: at least one window height on each side; preferred two (slide 43). Do not recolor, stretch, rotate, redraw or regenerate (slides 40, 47).
- Tagline: Building value. Delivering trust. Preserve periods, sentence case, once per composition; omit on forms (slide 45).
- Website direction: readable contrast, responsive type, real proof, one clear action (slide 63).
