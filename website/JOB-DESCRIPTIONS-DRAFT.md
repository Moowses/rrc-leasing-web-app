# RRC job descriptions - proposed drafts for HR review

These qualifications and job roles are proposed draft copy, not approved RRC hiring policy. The nine vacancy titles were supplied by RRC. HR should confirm the role scope, qualifications, preferred credentials, reporting lines, and any other hiring terms before publishing the final descriptions. No salary, work location, age, gender, or minimum years of experience have been assumed.

The text below matches the website vacancy data in vacancies.js. Any revision should be applied to that source and reflected here before publication.

## Project Engineer

Coordinate construction, renovation, and improvement projects across RRC properties.

### Qualifications

- Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering or a relevant engineering discipline; professional license preferred.
- Experience coordinating building construction, renovation, or fit-out projects.
- Ability to read plans, prepare quantity estimates, and monitor project costs and schedules.
- Strong site coordination, documentation, and contractor communication skills.

### Job roles

- Plan project activities, material requirements, budgets, and progress milestones.
- Coordinate contractors, suppliers, and internal teams throughout project delivery.
- Check site work against approved plans, specifications, and quality requirements.
- Track progress, costs, variations, and issues requiring management decisions.
- Organize inspections, punch lists, completion records, and project handover.

## Collections Officer

Support timely collection of rent and property charges through accurate records and professional tenant follow-up.

### Qualifications

- Degree or relevant training in Accountancy, Finance, Business Administration, or a related field.
- Experience in collections, accounts receivable, billing, or customer account handling.
- Comfortable using spreadsheets and accounting or property management systems.
- Clear communication, attention to detail, and discretion when handling tenant information.

### Job roles

- Monitor tenant balances, payment due dates, and overdue accounts.
- Send approved reminders and follow up on payment commitments professionally.
- Coordinate payment verification and account reconciliation with Accounting.
- Record collection activities, disputed charges, and agreed follow-up dates.
- Prepare collection reports and refer unresolved accounts for management action.

## Design Architect

Develop practical design solutions for RRC’s commercial and residential spaces.

### Qualifications

- Bachelor’s degree in Architecture and registration as a licensed architect.
- Portfolio showing building design, renovation, space planning, or fit-out work.
- Proficiency in architectural drafting, modeling, and presentation software.
- Ability to coordinate design details with engineers, contractors, and operations teams.

### Job roles

- Develop concept designs and layouts based on approved project requirements.
- Prepare drawings, specifications, material proposals, and design presentations.
- Coordinate architectural details with structural and building services plans.
- Review site conditions and address design questions during construction.
- Maintain drawing revisions and support design reviews and project handover.

## Maintenance Head

Lead maintenance operations to keep RRC properties reliable, serviceable, and ready for occupancy.

### Qualifications

- Relevant engineering degree, technical diploma, or equivalent facilities maintenance experience.
- Experience supervising building maintenance teams and external service providers.
- Working knowledge of electrical, plumbing, mechanical, and general building maintenance.
- Ability to prioritize work, manage maintenance budgets, and maintain service records.

### Job roles

- Develop preventive maintenance schedules and monitor their completion.
- Assign and track repairs, tenant service requests, and urgent maintenance work.
- Supervise technicians and contractors and check the quality of completed work.
- Control maintenance materials, spare parts, equipment, and operating costs.
- Report recurring defects and recommend repairs, replacements, or improvements.

## Fabrication Head

Lead fabrication and installation work for property repairs, improvements, and approved RRC projects.

### Qualifications

- Technical or vocational training in welding, metal fabrication, or a related trade.
- Experience supervising fabrication, workshop activities, and on-site installation.
- Ability to interpret drawings, take measurements, and prepare material requirements.
- Strong workmanship standards, team supervision, and workshop safety practices.

### Job roles

- Plan fabrication jobs, work assignments, schedules, and material requirements.
- Verify measurements and drawings before cutting, assembly, and installation.
- Supervise fabrication work and inspect dimensions, fit, finish, and workmanship.
- Monitor tools, equipment condition, material usage, and workshop housekeeping.
- Coordinate installations and maintain job progress and completion records.

## Leasing Supervisor

Lead leasing activities for RRC-owned properties, from vacancy marketing to tenant move-in.

### Qualifications

- Degree in Business Administration, Marketing, Real Estate Management, or a related field.
- Experience in commercial or residential leasing, with team coordination or supervision.
- Strong negotiation, tenant communication, and leasing documentation skills.
- Ability to monitor occupancy, leasing activity, and team performance using clear reports.

### Job roles

- Supervise inquiries, property viewings, application follow-up, and move-in coordination.
- Maintain accurate vacancy information and coordinate approved property marketing.
- Review applications and proposed lease terms for authorized management approval.
- Coordinate renewals, tenant concerns, and handovers with Accounting and Maintenance.
- Report occupancy, prospective tenants, upcoming expiries, and leasing priorities.

## Leasing Coordinator

Help prospective and current tenants through an organized, responsive leasing process.

### Qualifications

- Degree, diploma, or relevant training in Business Administration, Marketing, or a related field.
- Experience in customer service, leasing support, sales administration, or office coordination.
- Comfortable using email, spreadsheets, and digital application or property records.
- Organized, responsive, and careful when handling tenant documents and personal information.

### Job roles

- Respond to inquiries and provide approved property and rental information.
- Arrange viewings and coordinate schedules with applicants and the leasing team.
- Check applications for completeness and follow up on outstanding requirements.
- Update vacancy records, applicant status, and approved lease documentation.
- Coordinate move-in arrangements, renewals, and tenant requests with the responsible teams.

## Finance Head

Lead financial planning, reporting, and controls that support RRC’s property operations and investment decisions.

### Qualifications

- Bachelor’s degree in Accountancy, Finance, or a related field; CPA qualification preferred.
- Experience leading finance or accounting teams and reviewing management reports.
- Strong knowledge of budgeting, cash flow, financial controls, and account reconciliation.
- Ability to explain financial results and coordinate reporting and filing requirements.

### Job roles

- Lead budgeting, cash flow forecasting, and financial performance reporting.
- Review financial records, reconciliations, and the timely completion of period-end reports.
- Oversee receivables, payables, and payments within approved authority levels.
- Maintain financial controls and coordinate with external accountants, auditors, and advisers.
- Evaluate property and project financial results and recommend actions to management.

## Accounting Staff

Maintain accurate accounting records and support the daily financial transactions of RRC properties.

### Qualifications

- Degree or relevant training in Accountancy, Accounting Technology, or a related field.
- Knowledge of bookkeeping, supporting documents, and basic account reconciliation.
- Comfortable using spreadsheets and accounting software.
- Careful with figures, organized with records, and able to handle information confidentially.

### Job roles

- Record transactions and organize invoices, receipts, and other supporting documents.
- Prepare billing and payment documents for review and approval.
- Assist with bank, tenant account, and supplier account reconciliations.
- Coordinate payment records and discrepancies with Collections and Leasing.
- Support period-end schedules, reports, and the retrieval of records for review.
